Our Partners

Collaborating with organizations that share our vision for better transportation in Ghana.

Technology Partners

Expo

We build our mobile application using Expo and React Native, enabling us to deliver high-quality iOS and Android apps with a single codebase.

Supabase

Our backend infrastructure is powered by Supabase, providing us with a scalable PostgreSQL database, real-time subscriptions, and authentication services.

TomTom

We rely on TomTom's mapping and navigation services for accurate location data, route optimization, and geofencing capabilities.

Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to monitor app performance and user behavior, helping us continuously improve the user experience.

Community & Government Partners

Kumasi Metropolitan Assembly

We work closely with the KMA to ensure our services align with local transportation regulations and contribute to the city's mobility goals.

Ghana Private Road Transport Union (GPRTU)

We collaborate with GPRTU to engage with trotro operators and drivers, ensuring our services meet the needs of transport professionals.

Kumasi Technical University

We partner with KTU on research initiatives and provide internship opportunities for students in computer science and engineering.

MTN Ghana

We integrate with MTN Mobile Money for seamless payment processing, enabling users to pay for rides using Ghana's most popular mobile money service.

How to Partner With Us

We're always looking to collaborate with organizations that share our mission of improving transportation in Ghana. Whether you're a technology company, government agency, nonprofit, or private enterprise, we'd love to explore how we can work together.

Potential partnership areas include:

  • Technology integration and API partnerships
  • Joint research and data sharing initiatives
  • Community outreach and educational programs
  • Infrastructure and urban planning collaboration
  • Payment processing and financial services integration
