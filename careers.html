About TrotroOS

TrotroOS is a unified real-time mobility platform serving Kumasi, Ghana. We connect passengers with trotro mates, offer shared rides through TrotroRide, and provide tools for station operators and drivers. Our mission is to make daily transportation clearer, safer, and more efficient for everyone.

We believe in building technology that serves real needs. Our team is comprised of engineers, designers, and problem-solvers who are passionate about using technology to improve daily life in Ghana.
