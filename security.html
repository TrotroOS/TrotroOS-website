Security

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Our Security Commitment

At TrotroOS, we take the security and privacy of our users' data seriously. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information and continuously work to improve our security posture.

Data Protection

We employ multiple layers of security to protect user data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between our apps and servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive data stored in our databases is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
  • Secure Authentication: We use industry-standard authentication protocols with rate limiting and brute force protection.
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct regular security assessments and penetration testing.

Privacy by Design

We follow privacy-by-design principles in all our development:

  • Data minimization: We only collect data necessary for providing our services.
  • Purpose limitation: We use data only for the purposes specified in our privacy policy.
  • Transparency: We clearly communicate what data we collect and how we use it.
  • User control: Users can access, correct, and delete their personal data.

Vulnerability Reporting

We welcome security researchers and ethical hackers to help us keep TrotroOS secure. If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in our systems, please report it responsibly.

How to Report: Please send a detailed description of the vulnerability to security@trotroos.app.

What to Include: To help us investigate efficiently, please include:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue (if applicable).
  • The version of the app or website where you encountered the issue.
  • Any relevant screenshots, logs, or proof-of-concept code.

Our Commitment: We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days and provide regular updates on our investigation. We offer recognition and, where appropriate, monetary rewards for validated security reports.

Security Practices

Secure Development Lifecycle

We integrate security practices throughout our development process, from design to deployment and maintenance.

Regular Security Training

All employees receive regular security training to stay current with threats and best practices.

Incident Response Plan

We maintain a comprehensive incident response plan to quickly address any security incidents.

Third-Party Security

We carefully vet and monitor all third-party services and dependencies for security risks.

Compliance & Certifications

We are committed to meeting relevant security and privacy standards:

  • GDPR compliance for users in the European Union
  • Adherence to Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)
  • Regular compliance assessments and audits
