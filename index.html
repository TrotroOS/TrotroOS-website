How a trip works

  1. 1

    Choose corridor

    Pick the route you take daily — same names you use at the station.

  2. 2

    Book or bid

    Reserve on a live mate, send a fare bid, or wait in queue until one matches.

  3. 3

    Board & pay

    Show your verification code, follow GPS, pay with MoMo or on board.

Safety that holds up after the trip

When a mate accepts your reservation, both sides get a random six-digit pickup code. It's proof of handoff if a fare or seat dispute comes up later.
