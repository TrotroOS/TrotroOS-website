How it works

From queue to boarding — without guesswork.

  1. 01

    Pick your corridor

    Tech Junction, Ayeduase, Kejetia, KNUST — the routes you already use every day.

  2. 02

    Book, bid, or queue

    Reserve a mate seat, send a Bid & Ride offer, or wait until a live trip matches.

  3. 03

    Track & travel

    Follow live GPS, show your pickup verification code, pay with MoMo when prompted.

Trust & safety

Designed for disputes, not just demos.

When a mate accepts your reservation, both sides get a random 6-digit pickup code — proof of handoff if fare or seat disputes come up later.

  • Trip Guardian browser sharing for trusted contacts
  • Trust score and verification tiers on profiles
  • SOS and emergency services quick links
  • Crash reporting with user opt-out in privacy settings
