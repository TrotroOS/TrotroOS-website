    Built for how Ghana actually moves

    TrotroOS is not a ride-hail replacement. It is a layer on top of the vans, queues, and mates already running Kumasi every day.

    How a trip works

    1. 1

      Check demand & pick a corridor

      See the heat map on Find Ride. Tech Junction → Ayeduase. Kejetia → KNUST. The names you already shout at the stop.

    2. 2

      Book, bid, or queue

      Reserve on a live mate, lock your fare for five minutes, send a cedi bid, or wait until a van on your route matches.

    3. 3

      Board, chat & pay

      After accept, chat with your mate and follow live GPS. Show your six-digit code to board. Pay MoMo or cash — your choice.

    Safety that holds up after the trip

    When a mate accepts your reservation, both sides get a random six-digit pickup code. It is proof of handoff if a fare or seat dispute comes up later.
