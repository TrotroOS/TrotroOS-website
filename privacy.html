Privacy Policy

Effective July 14, 2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how TrotroOS collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our mobility platform.

01 · Overview

TrotroOS is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to information collected through our mobile app, website, and customer support channels. By using TrotroOS, you consent to the practices described here.

02 · Information We Collect

• Account information: name, email, phone number, profile photo, role, and verification details. • Trip information: origin, destination, bookings, ratings, and trip history. • Location data: precise location when you search for rides, during active trips, or when drivers broadcast GPS (subject to your device permissions and in-app settings). • Payment data: transaction references, payment method type, and fare amounts (we do not store full mobile money PINs). • Device & usage data: app version, language, crash logs, and interaction analytics to improve reliability.

03 · How We Use Information

We use your information to: • Match you with available rides and drivers • Process reservations, payments, and receipts • Provide live tracking, Trip Guardian, and safety features • Calculate trust scores and display ratings • Send trip updates, reminders, and service notifications • Detect fraud, enforce policies, and comply with legal obligations • Improve our products and develop new features

04 · How We Share Information

We share limited information with: • Drivers and co-passengers: name, pickup/drop-off, and trip status needed to complete your ride • Payment processors: to facilitate MoMo, GhQR, and other payment methods • Service providers: cloud hosting, analytics, and support tools under contractual safeguards • Authorities: when required by law or to protect safety We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

05 · Location Data

Location is essential for ride matching and safety. You can control location permissions through your device settings and certain in-app privacy controls. Disabling location may limit core features such as live tracking and nearby ride discovery.

06 · Data Retention

We retain account and trip records for as long as your account is active and as needed for legal, safety, and dispute-resolution purposes. You may request export or deletion of your data through Profile → Data & Privacy, subject to exceptions required by law.

07 · Security

We implement administrative, technical, and organizational measures—including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure—to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong password and report suspicious activity.

08 · Your Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. Contact us at trotroapp@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

09 · Children

TrotroOS is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us to request deletion.

10 · International Transfers

Your data may be processed on servers located outside Ghana through our cloud providers. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable data protection requirements.

11 · Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or email where required.
